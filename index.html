Community Bulletin & News

Council Monthly Update

Our local city council is introducing several green initiatives this month. We are planning to expand public parks, add more bicycle lanes, and host bi-weekly recycling drives. Residents are highly encouraged to participate in these programs to make our neighborhood cleaner and more sustainable for future generations.

Additionally, funding has been approved for the upcoming autumn festival. Volunteers are needed for setting up food stalls, managing guest registrations, and coordinating local artist performances. If you are interested in volunteering, please drop a message using our registration form or visit the town administration office during standard working hours.
